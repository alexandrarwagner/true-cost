General tips

HousingLive a few stops further outRent usually drops away from the campus core. Many students share a place a stop or two out and ride in.
HousingPlan around the lease startLeases near a campus tend to start on the same day each year. Students moving out often give away furniture that week, so wait before buying.
Living costsCook together, shop in bulkSharing staples with flatmates and cooking together goes much further than eating out.
Moving moneySend fewer, larger transfersEach transfer has fees, and banks often add a markup to the rate. Compare against the mid-market rate before you send.
