How we work it out.

Five costs, added up in dollars, then turned into your currency at today’s rate.

CostHow we work it outSourceType
Tuition Your tuition per year, times the number of years. You type it in, from your offer letter. Your school: the offer letter or Form I-20 Official data
Housing Typical rent near your school, times 12 months a year, because most leases run through the summer. US Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), Fair Market Rents by area Government data
Living costs Food, transport, phone, books and health insurance, per month, times the months you’re there. Your school’s own cost-of-attendance figures (College Scorecard) Official data
One-off costs Things you pay once: the visa application, the SEVIS fee, deposits and flights. US State Department (travel.state.gov) for visa fees; fmjfee.com for the SEVIS fee; our estimate for flights Our estimate
Moving money The fees banks and transfer services charge each time your family sends money. Published fees from banks and money-transfer services Our estimate
Exchange rate Today’s rate turns dollars into your currency. The range shows how much the rate moved over past two-year stretches. We never predict where it goes next. Today’s reference rate and past rates from Frankfurter (European Central Bank reference rates) Official data
